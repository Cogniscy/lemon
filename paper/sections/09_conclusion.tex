\section{Conclusion}
We introduced \lemonscore{}, a source-directed factor-level coverage framework for graph--text evaluation. The method decomposes RDF predicates into weighted semantic factors and scores whether each factor is reflected in the text. In a WebNLG pilot, \lemonscore{} exposed the difference between high entity-label overlap and lower predicate-level semantic coverage. Error-driven expansion reduced missing decomposition rate from 0.5455 to 0.0 and raised \lemonscore{} coverage from 0.3683 to 0.7915, while surface lexical baselines stayed unchanged.

The result supports the main claim of the pilot: factor-level semantic coverage provides interpretable diagnostic information that is not available from holistic reference metrics or simple lexical overlap. Future work will replace synthetic adjudication with human expert validation, add paraphrase-sensitive evidence, and compare the metric against human data-coverage judgments at larger scale.
