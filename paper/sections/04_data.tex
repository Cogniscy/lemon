\section{Data and Experimental Resources}
\subsection{WebNLG pilot}
The experiments use a stratified English WebNLG pilot. The train split contains 200 examples and the development split contains 100 examples. Both splits cover 16 WebNLG categories. The development set contains 308 graph edges. The pilot is intentionally small: it validates the end-to-end scoring pipeline, exposes interpretable failure modes, and allows manual inspection of perturbation manifests before scaling to larger WebNLG subsets or domain-specific graphs.

\subsection{Predicate decomposition resources}
A seed factor inventory was built from training predicates, node labels, categories, and predicate contexts. LLM-assisted candidate generation was then evaluated against the seed reference with two models. This step tests whether general LLMs can propose plausible factor decompositions under a fixed schema; it is not human validation.

A synthetic adjudicator was applied to 29 predicates. It modified 19 decisions, accepted 10 seed decisions, and produced a mean confidence of 0.830. LLM candidate coverage was 0.621 because 18 of the 29 adjudicated predicates had LLM candidates. The resulting resource is useful for developing the framework, but it remains synthetic and provisional.
