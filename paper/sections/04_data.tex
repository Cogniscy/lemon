\section{Data and Experimental Resources}
\subsection{WebNLG sample}
The WebNLG experiments use a stratified English sample from the RDF-to-text benchmark introduced above. Each item pairs a small DBpedia-derived graph with one or more English verbalizations. The train split contains 200 examples and the development split contains 100 examples; both cover 16 WebNLG categories. The development set contains 308 graph edges. The sample is small by design: it keeps the scoring pipeline auditable while still exposing relation-level errors that entity overlap can hide.

\subsection{Biomedical transfer resources}
The biomedical transfer checks use two relation extraction corpora converted into the same graph--text JSONL format. DrugProt is a BioCreative VII resource for chemical--gene/protein relations with 13 relation types and 24,526 manually annotated relations \cite{miranda2023drugprot}. BC5CDR is a BioCreative V collection of 1500 PubMed articles annotated for chemicals, diseases, and chemical-induced disease (CID) relations; it contains 3116 chemical--disease interactions \cite{li2016bc5cdr}. In our processed subsets, DrugProt provides 500 train abstracts with 3588 edges, and BC5CDR provides 500 train and 500 development documents with 985 and 961 CID edges.

Biomedical text stresses the dimensions that motivate LEMON. In scientific abstracts, a change from inhibition to activation, a reversed chemical--protein relation, or a weakened causal claim can alter the interpretation of the result. Literal evidence such as dosages, measurements, and experimental conditions also becomes important when text is translated into a graph. DrugProt and BC5CDR are therefore used as diagnostic transfer settings: they test role, direction, polarity, causality, and evidence under domain conditions where surface entity overlap is not enough. We do not train or claim a new biomedical relation extraction system.

\subsection{Predicate-factor inventories}
The experiments use fixed JSON predicate-factor inventories. Each inventory contains a controlled factor vocabulary and one weighted decomposition per predicate. WebNLG uses 121 predicate decompositions and covers all 119 predicate labels observed in the evaluation files. DrugProt contains 13 biomedical relation decompositions and covers the 12 relation types observed in the processed subset. BC5CDR is intentionally a single-relation setting: its inventory decomposes the document-level chemical-induced disease relation into chemical exposure, disease outcome, causality, document-level evidence, and chemical-to-disease direction.

The inventories are rule-based diagnostic resources. Earlier LLM-generated decomposition candidates are kept as development material, but they are not treated as expert gold and are not used as the main evidence in the scoring tables below. The validator checks that coverage is complete for the current evaluation files and that each predicate decomposition has normalized weights.
