\section{Limitations}

The present pilot does not contain human expert validation of factor decompositions. Synthetic LLM adjudication is used only as a bootstrap and is subject to known LLM-as-judge biases. The expanded decomposition dictionary is deterministic but not a gold standard.

The current coverage scorer is lexical. It handles explicit labels and known predicate cues, but it can miss paraphrases, implicit relations, and discourse-level evidence. Future versions should add embedding- or entailment-based evidence while retaining factor-level auditability.

Finally, the WebNLG pilot is small. Larger-scale experiments should measure correlation with human data-coverage judgments and compare against FactSpotter, Data-QuestEval, and neural reference-based metrics.
