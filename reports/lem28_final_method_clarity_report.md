## Interpretation
The paper should now defend the following claim:

> MINE-style and triple matching are harsher coarse detectors, while LEMON-Factor supplies higher-resolution factor-localized diagnosis.

It should not claim that LEMON-Factor is universally better or always produces larger perturbation drops.
